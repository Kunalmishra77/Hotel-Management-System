# Product

## Client
**Woodpecker Apartments & Suites Pvt. Ltd.** — a growing serviced-apartments / suites operator in India, expanding to multiple properties and branches.

## Vision
One mobile-first platform, usable by **non-technical staff** on phone/tablet/laptop, that runs daily operations end-to-end: reservations, front desk, guest CRM, GST billing, expenses, staff, housekeeping, maintenance, analytics, and automated guest communication — across multiple properties from a single dashboard, with AI assistance.

## Primary users (see `user-roles.md`)
- **Reception** — reservations, check-in/out, billing. The highest-frequency user. Optimise for speed.
- **Manager / Owner** — occupancy, revenue, profit, multi-property overview.
- **Accounts** — invoicing, payments, expenses, exports, accounting sync.
- **Housekeeping** — updates room status from a phone, often on weak Wi-Fi (offline-capable).
- **Maintenance** — logs and closes maintenance jobs; preventive reminders.
- **Administrator** — users, roles, properties, configuration.

## Outcomes that define success (the "why" behind every feature)
1. **Less manual work** — a booking-to-invoice flow with no double entry.
2. **Complete guest relationship memory** — permanent CRM + history, searchable instantly.
3. **Money is always correct** — GST-compliant invoices, folio never drifts, no lost charges.
4. **No overbooking** — availability is authoritative across direct + OTA channels.
5. **Real-time business insight** — occupancy, ARR, RevPAR, profit at a glance, per property.
6. **Automated communication** — the right message on WhatsApp/Email/SMS at the right moment.
7. **Learnable in minutes** — reception, housekeeping, accounts onboard with minimal training.

## Product principles
- **Speed for the front desk beats feature richness.** "Generate an invoice in under a minute" is a hard requirement, not a nice-to-have.
- **Mobile is the primary surface**, not an afterthought.
- **The system should feel trustworthy with money and guest data** — auditable, encrypted, never silently wrong.
- **Competitive bar:** MEWS / Cloudbeds / eZee-class capability. We reach it by getting the domain core right (folio, availability, night audit, events), not by cutting corners.
