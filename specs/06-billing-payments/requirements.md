# 06 · Billing & Payments — Requirements

> The money core. Source: client doc §5. Read with `.claude/rules/business-rules.md` §5–15 (folio, GST, night audit), `rules/data-model.md` (money/paise/append-only), `rules/compliance.md` (GST retention), `rules/reporting.md` (revenue definition), `rules/non-functional-requirements.md`, `prisma/schema.prisma`. This module is the single authority for money; every figure it exposes reconciles exactly with the folio.

## Purpose & scope
Own the **folio** (one running account per reservation), all **charges** and **payments** as append-only lines, **GST tax invoices** with gap-free per-property/FY numbering, and the **posting side of the night-audit close**. Balance is always derived, never stored. All charges/payments/reversals/invoices are immutable rows; corrections are new reversing entries.

**In scope:** folio creation & lifecycle (per-reservation folios **and** the walk-in/house `DIRECT_SALE` folio for POS with no reservation — `Folio.reservationId` is nullable, `Folio.kind` = `RESERVATION|DIRECT_SALE`); posting charges (room, F&B, laundry, transfer, taxi, extra-bed, POS pass-through, misc) with per-line GST (CGST/SGST/IGST); discounts as negative lines; reversals of charges; payments in every `PaymentMode` including **split** tender and **advance**; refunds as reversing entries; walk-in POS settlement (`settlePosSaleDirect`); online payment via `PaymentProvider` (sandbox default); GST tax-invoice generation (B2B & B2C) with sequential gap-free numbering; the room-night charge posting invoked by night audit (14); revenue/outstanding queries consumed by 08/14; checkout balance gate consumed by 03.

**Out of scope:** reservation lifecycle & the checkout transition itself (03 — calls `billing.getBalance`), night-audit orchestration/snapshot & business-date roll (14 — calls `billing.postRoomCharges`), POS order capture (19 — calls `billing.postFolioCharge`; walk-in POS settlement goes through `settlePosSaleDirect`), corporate AR statements/aging (25 — reads the receivable this module records via `corporateReceivable`, never a foreign SELECT), expense accounting (07), accounting-provider push (22 — consumes `InvoiceIssued`/`PaymentReceived`/`PaymentRefunded`).

## Dependencies
- **Tier 0:** 00-platform (auth, tenancy, events, audit, outbox/inbox, object storage), 01-property-management (property state, GSTIN, invoice prefix/code), 02-room-inventory (`RoomCategory.hsnSac`, tariff for GST slab).
- **Tier 1:** 03-reservations (folio owner reservation; checkout gate), 04-guest-crm (bill-to name/GSTIN/state).
- **Tier 2 peer:** 07-expense-management (independent; both feed 08).
- **Upstream callers:** 14-analytics/night-audit (`postRoomCharges`, closed-date guard), 19-pos (`postFolioCharge` for in-house pass-through; `settlePosSaleDirect` + `reverseFolioLine` for walk-in sale & void), 25-corporate-crm (calls `reserveCredit` back into 06's settlement tx; reads receivable via `corporateReceivable`), 24 (resolved rate already on the reservation).
- **Downstream consumers:** 08-profit-reports & 14-analytics (revenue/outstanding queries + `FolioCharged`/`PaymentReceived`), 05-guest-history (`guestBilling` query + the full folio-mutating event set), 12-communications (receipts, invoice copies, due reminders), 22-accounting-sync (`InvoiceIssued`, `PaymentReceived`, `PaymentRefunded`), 25-corporate-crm (`corporateReceivable` + `CorporateReceivableChanged`).

## Data owned
`Folio` (with `kind` = `RESERVATION|DIRECT_SALE` and nullable `reservationId`, both confirmed present in canonical schema), `FolioLine` (append-only), `Payment` (append-only, incl. refunds), `InvoiceSeries`, `Invoice`. Money columns on `FolioLine`/`Payment`/`Invoice` (and `Corporate.creditLimitPaise`/`receivablePaise`) are **`BigInt` paise** in the canonical schema (accumulating totals); per-unit rates and tax components are `Int` paise. Reads: `Reservation`, `Property` (state, gstin, code, `currentBusinessDate`), `RoomCategory` (hsnSac, tariff band), `Guest`/`Corporate` (bill-to identity, GSTIN, place-of-supply state). Tax rate/slab config is code-defined in `lib/constants/gst.ts` (band→bps, HSN/SAC→rate), never hard-coded per call (business-rules §11).

## Functional requirements (EARS)
- **FR-1 (ubiquitous):** The system shall maintain exactly one `Folio` per `Reservation`, property-scoped, created idempotently by `ensureFolio(reservationId)` (called by 03 on confirm and again on check-in — the second call is a no-op).
- **FR-2 (ubiquitous):** The system shall record every charge and payment as an **append-only** row; no posted `FolioLine`, `Payment`, or `Invoice` is ever updated or deleted in a way that changes financial meaning (data-model append-only; business-rules §7).
- **FR-3 (ubiquitous):** The system shall always **derive** folio balance = `Σ(line.amount + line.cgst + line.sgst + line.igst) − Σ(non-refund payment.amountPaise) + Σ(refund payment.amountPaise)`; because `Payment.amountPaise` is stored positive and refunds are flagged (`isRefund`), the derivation **adds refund amounts back** to the balance. Balance is never a stored editable column (business-rules §6).
- **FR-4 (event):** When a charge is posted, the system shall compute GST for that line by HSN/SAC and applicable rate, and determine the split by **place of supply vs the property's state**. For **accommodation and all on-premise services** — `ROOM`, `FOOD`, `LAUNDRY`, `AIRPORT_TRANSFER`, `TAXI`, `KITCHEN`, `EXTRA_BED`, `POS` — the place of supply is **always the property's own state** (accommodation/restaurant services are supplied where the property is located, per IGST Act §12(3)/§12(4)), so the split is **always CGST+SGST regardless of the guest's or corporate bill-to state**; `FolioLine.placeOfSupplyState` is stamped with the property state for these lines. Only the **rare genuine inter-state supply** — a supply whose place of supply legitimately differs from the property state (e.g. goods dispatched inter-state, or a distinct service not consumed on the premises) — is billed as **IGST**. The system shall store the tax components on the line, emit `FolioCharged`, and write audit (business-rules §10).
- **FR-5 (event):** When night audit (14) runs for a business date, the system shall post exactly one **room-night** `FolioLine` (type `ROOM`) per in-house folio for that date, **idempotently** (a re-run posts nothing new), pricing at the reservation's resolved nightly rate and its tariff GST slab (business-rules §11, §14). Idempotency is enforced by the **partial unique index** `UNIQUE(folioId, businessDate) WHERE type='ROOM'` on `FolioLine` (database-setup.md) — a re-run's duplicate insert conflicts and is skipped, not double-posted.
- **FR-6 (event):** When a discount is applied, the system shall record a negative `DISCOUNT` line. A discount is **pre-tax by default**: it reduces the **taxable value** and carries **negated proportional CGST/SGST** (or IGST) so tax is charged on the net taxable amount — the `DISCOUNT` line's `amountPaise` and its `cgstPaise`/`sgstPaise`/`igstPaise` are all negative and the whole line reconciles into `folioBalance`. A discount may alternatively be recorded as **financial-only** (post-tax): a negative `DISCOUNT` line with **zero tax components**, reducing the payable total without altering the taxable value. If the discount exceeds the actor's role threshold (`SecuritySettings.discountThresholdPaise`) it shall require `folio:discount` and write an audited override, else reject; emit `DiscountApplied` (business-rules §7; FR-19 of 03).
- **FR-7 (unwanted):** If a posted line must be corrected, then the system shall never edit or delete it — the `reverseFolioLine(lineId, reason)` action shall insert a `REVERSAL` line referencing `reversalOfId` with the exact negated amount and negated tax components (business-rules §7). This append-only reversal is the mechanism for **POS voids and corrections** (19 calls it on `PosOrderVoided`); if the reversed charge is already on an issued invoice, correction flows through the **credit-note path** (FR-21), never a silent edit.
- **FR-8 (event):** When a payment is received, the system shall record one `Payment` per tender, all sharing one `Payment.settlementBatchId`; a **split** settlement shall write multiple `Payment` rows in one transaction whose amounts sum to the settled amount; it shall emit exactly one `PaymentReceived` per settlement batch whose payload carries `settlementBatchId` and `tenders[]` (`{mode, amountPaise}` per row, per the domain-event catalog) so 22-accounting and 12-communications reconcile the batch from the event without re-reading `Payment` rows.
- **FR-9 (state):** While a reservation is `CONFIRMED` or `IN_HOUSE`, the system shall accept advance and interim payments; an advance reduces the derived balance. **06 owns the money** (the `Payment` rows are the source of truth); `Reservation.advancePaise` is a **display snapshot written by 03**, which reacts to the `PaymentReceived` event to recompute it — 06 never writes that column. The snapshot shall reconcile with the sum of non-refund advance payments derived here.
- **FR-10 (event):** When a refund is issued, the system shall record a `Payment` with **`amountPaise` stored POSITIVE and `isRefund=true`** (direction is carried by the flag, never by a negative amount — see also the `CHECK(amount_paise >= 0)` constraint), and/or a `REVERSAL` line; require `folio:refund`; emit `PaymentRefunded`; and never refund more than the **net amount paid**. Net paid = `Σ(non-refund payments) − Σ(refund payments)`; refund direction is applied in the derivation (`folioBalance` adds refunds back), so a refund *increases* the outstanding balance by its amount.
- **FR-11 (event):** When an online payment is chosen, the system shall create the order through the `PaymentProvider` interface (sandbox/mock when no live credentials) and record the `Payment` only on a **signature-verified** webhook, deduped via `IntegrationInbox` (integrations.md; api-conventions webhooks).
- **FR-12 (event):** When an invoice is generated, the system shall, in **one SHORT transaction**, derive the financial year from the invoice date (**FY starts 1 April** — a date in Jan–Mar belongs to the prior April-started FY), **create the `InvoiceSeries` row on the first invoice** of that `(propertyId, financialYear)` if none exists, allocate the next number under row lock, compute statutory totals, insert the `Invoice` row (with `pdfObjectKey` **null**), emit `InvoiceIssued`, and COMMIT. It shall **render/attach the PDF AFTER commit** — the PDF is rendered to object storage and `pdfObjectKey` (nullable) is updated in a follow-up write — so a slow render never holds the numbering lock (business-rules §12–13). **Credit notes draw the same `InvoiceSeries`** (FR-21).
- **FR-13 (ubiquitous):** The system shall keep invoice numbering **sequential, gap-free, and never reused** per `(propertyId, financialYear)` via `InvoiceSeries.nextNumber` consumed under row lock (business-rules §13).
- **FR-14 (unwanted):** If the invoice-numbering transaction fails or rolls back, then the series number shall not be consumed (no gaps), and no `Invoice` row shall exist for it. If instead the **post-commit PDF render** fails, the `Invoice` remains valid with `pdfObjectKey` null and the render is retried (idempotent) — a render failure never creates a numbering gap.
- **FR-15 (state):** While a business date is closed by night audit (14), the system shall reject new or back-dated postings dated to that closed date. The current open day is read from **`Property.currentBusinessDate`** (the night-audit writer of that field) — a posting whose `businessDate` is earlier than `currentBusinessDate` is rejected (`CLOSED_DATE_POSTING`), except an **audited adjustment** posted to `currentBusinessDate` referencing the original line (business-rules §14–15).
- **FR-16 (ubiquitous):** The system shall render a **GST tax invoice** carrying: gap-free number, property GSTIN, customer name (and GSTIN for B2B), place of supply, per-line HSN/SAC, taxable value, tax breakup (CGST/SGST or IGST), grand total, and **total in words** (business-rules §12).
- **FR-17 (event):** When a folio is settled on `CORPORATE_CREDIT`, the system shall call **`25.reserveCredit(corporateId, amountPaise)` ATOMICALLY inside the same settlement transaction** — an atomic check-and-increment under a **row lock** on the corporate row, not a check-then-act (which would race two concurrent settlements past the limit). Within that one transaction it records the settlement `Payment`; if `reserveCredit` reports the limit would be exceeded, the whole transaction rolls back (`CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`) and nothing persists. It emits `CorporateReceivableChanged`; 25 reads the receivable through this module's **`corporateReceivable(corporateId)`** query, never a foreign SELECT.
- **FR-18 (event):** When 03 requests a checkout balance, the system shall return the derived balance so 03 can block checkout unless settled or `folio:defer` (business-rules §5–6; FR-10 of 03).
- **FR-19 (ubiquitous):** The system shall store all money as integer **paise**, compute with Decimal.js, and round **half-up to the paisa at line level**; intra-state tax splits into CGST=SGST each rounded independently (business-rules §8, §10).
- **FR-20 (event):** When a positive balance remains after checkout or at night-audit close, the system shall emit `PaymentDueDetected` so 12 can send reminders.
- **FR-21 (unwanted):** If an invoice void is requested, then the system shall not delete or renumber it — it shall record a `CREDIT_NOTE` (`Invoice.type=CREDIT_NOTE`, `cancelsInvoiceId` referencing the original) drawing the **same `InvoiceSeries`** so numbering stays gap-free, and require `invoice:void` (🔒, audited). This is also the correction path for a reversed line already carried on an issued invoice (FR-7).
- **FR-22 (ubiquitous):** The system shall property-scope, server-side authorize, audit, and emit the domain event for every mutation (business-rules §20; security.md).
- **FR-23 (unwanted):** If a charge/payment/refund amount is ≤ 0, or a refund would exceed net paid, or a split's parts do not sum to the requested total, then the system shall reject at validation and persist nothing.
- **FR-24 (event):** When 08/14 request revenue or outstanding figures, the system shall return category-split, **net-of-discount, tax-excluded** revenue and derived outstanding balances that reconcile exactly with the folios (reporting.md Money).
- **FR-25 (event):** When a walk-in POS sale with **no reservation** is settled (19 calls `settlePosSaleDirect`), the system shall obtain the property's `DIRECT_SALE` folio via **`ensureDirectSaleFolio(propertyId)`** — a folio-less/house folio where `Folio.reservationId` is null and `Folio.kind = DIRECT_SALE`, created idempotently (one open direct-sale/house folio reused per property) — post the sale line(s) with GST (place of supply = property state, FR-4), record the `Payment`, generate a **gap-free GST invoice** through the same numbering path (FR-12/13), and return `{invoiceId, paymentId}` in one flow.
- **FR-26 (ubiquitous):** The system shall expose **`corporateReceivable(corporateId)`** as this module's query so 25 reads the corporate receivable it records (FR-17) without a foreign SELECT into billing tables; the returned figure reconciles with the settlement `Payment` rows and the `CorporateReceivableChanged` events.

## Non-functional (cited)
Invoice action completes and PDF renders **< 3s**; the open-guest→generate→present flow achievable in **< 60s** of staff time (§5, NFR). Charge/payment mutations feel instant via optimistic UI, server confirm p95 **< 800ms**. No lost/duplicated money under concurrency (correctness > availability): invoice numbering and payment posting are transaction-safe. 1M+ folio lines stay within budget via indexes + pagination + snapshots. (`rules/non-functional-requirements.md`)
